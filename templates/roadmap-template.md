Auditor during consolidation.

## Prioritization Criteria

### Priority Levels

- **P0 - Immediate**: Critical findings requiring immediate attention.
- **P1 - Short-term**: High findings to address within 30 days.
- **P2 - Medium-term**: Medium findings to address within 90 days.
- **P3 - Long-term**: Low and informational findings to address within 180 days.
