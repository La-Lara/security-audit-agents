# Finding Template

This template defines the structure for individual findings generated by
specialized auditors.

## Identifier

Format: `[PREFIX]-XXX`

Allowed prefixes:

- `SEC` for Security findings;
- `MON` for Monitoring findings;
- `OPS` for Operations findings;
- `COMP` for Compliance findings;
- `OBS` for Observability findings.

---

## Title

Short and objective description of the issue.

---

## Description

Explain the issue, gap, or weakness observed.

---

## Evidence

List supporting evidence:

- file paths;
- configuration references;
- implementation details;
- documentation references.

---

## Evidence Classification

One of:

- EVIDENCED;
- PARTIALLY_EVIDENCED;
- NOT_EVIDENCED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE.

---

## Risk

Describe the potential impact or risk.

---

## Severity

One of:

- Critical;
- High;
- Medium;
- Low;
- Informational.

---

## Status

One of:

- CONFIRMED;
- POTENTIAL;
- NOT_TESTED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE;
- FALSE_POSITIVE.

---

## Recommendation

Describe recommended actions to address the issue.

---

## Validation Suggestions

Describe how the finding could be validated.

Do not claim validation was performed.

---

## Framework Mapping

Optional. Map to relevant framework controls when applicable.

Do not force mappings.
