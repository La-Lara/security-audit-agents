# Risk Register Template

This template defines the structure for the risk register generated by the Lead
Auditor during consolidation.

## Risk Register Summary

Provide a high-level summary of identified risks.

---

## Risk Entries

### RISK-001

#### Title

Short and objective description.

#### Related Findings

List associated finding identifiers.

#### Risk Description

Describe the risk in detail.

#### Impact

Describe potential business or technical impact.

#### Likelihood

One of:

- High;
- Medium;
- Low.

Justify based on evidence.

#### Severity

One of:

- Critical;
- High;
- Medium;
- Low;
- Informational.

#### Status

One of:

- OPEN;
- MITIGATED;
- ACCEPTED;
- CLOSED.

#### Owner

Assign responsible party when known.

#### Mitigation

Describe recommended mitigation actions.

#### Timeline

Suggest remediation timeline based on severity.
